# Research and engineering reports

These reports preserve individual experiments and diagnoses. Start with the
[current guides](../README.md) for operating instructions.

## Radio and transport

- `ble-c6-*`: historical BLE delivery, signal quality, and timing.
- `espnow-*`: gateway transport, receiver scheduling, throughput, and endurance.
- `gateway-*`, `receiver-expiry-*`, and `usb-reference-overload-*`: buffering
  and recovery investigations.
- `loss-15min-*` and `room-transport-*`: measured transport performance.

## Models and analysis

- [Fall model pilots](fall-model-pilots.md): retained results from the removed
  two- and four-receiver CNN experiment notebooks.
- `live-model-diagnosis-*` and `phase-model-evaluation-*`: exploratory model
  diagnosis and collection-phase evaluation.
- `gpu-sequence-tuning-*`, `pytorch-cpu-vs-tinygrad-*`,
  `sequence-optimization-*`, `sequence-segment-store-*`, and
  `tinygrad-training-optimization-*`: training and sequence pipeline work.
- `amplitude-sequence-*`: sequence-aligned amplitude processing.
