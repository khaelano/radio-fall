# Fifteen-minute room loss test, 4 November 2026

The system completed a fixed 900-second headless observation after a 30-second
warm-up. Automatic recovery remained enabled. One reference interruption split
the observation into two radio runs, so this is not an
uninterrupted transport qualification. The clock did not restart after recovery.

The configuration remained 120 Hz/channel-44 HE20 sensing, channel-11 HT20 MCS3
LGI uploads, 125 ms slots, 96-record queues, 750 ms retention and no application
ACKs. Only the gateway used laptop USB data. No firmware was flashed. The
companion queue-lock source fix remains undeployed. The user reported the
farthest receiver about 5 m from the gateway; positions and power were not
independently measured. There was no GUI, camera, classifier or human activity
protocol in this test.

Receiver transport IDs increment after eligible CSI capture and before upload
queue admission. For each unchanged receiver boot, the denominator is the
inclusive ID span from its first to last delivered capture within the measurement;
missing IDs inside that span are post-capture loss. This excludes sensing that
was never captured, including planned off-channel time, and does not observe
loss outside the span edges. Recovery queue clearing, failed uploads and expiry
can all contribute. It does not assign an individual missing ID to a cause.
Exact bounds, counts and boot identities are in `loss-summary.json`.

Expiry increases use first/last received status counters within the window and
therefore have slightly different boundaries from the capture-ID spans. They
must not be equated to the loss numerator. `Dropped` counter increases summed
to 0; this does not cover expiry or all recovery discards. Receiver queue
reset also clears retained records, and its separate `epoch_dropped` field is
not included in the current status `Dropped` total.

Usable delivery excludes nonzero driver receive status and uses authoritative
measured sensing rounds, regardless of the per-destination send-mask bit. The
capture path validates transmitter/profile; a missing destination submission bit
is not grounds to discard valid CSI. Rates divide by the full 900 seconds,
including interruptions. All 349,772 usable delivered records were subsecond
after including clock uncertainty; the maximum conservative delay was 832.71 ms.
The saved summary separately
reports missing submissions, unsubmitted sensing opportunities, one-second rate
distributions, and longest receiver gaps. Unsubmitted opportunities combine
planned omissions and immediate submission failures; sequence gaps alone do not
identify their cause. Latency adds recorded clock uncertainty to the observed
capture-to-application acceptance delay.

## Interruptions and simultaneous availability

The longest interval between usable host acceptances across all receivers was
7.582 seconds. The longest gap between delivered usable capture timestamps
across all receivers was 7.627 seconds. These measure delivered-data silence;
they do not prove every receiver was physically
off-channel throughout that interval.

The original per-run 50 Hz availability journals retain their 900 ms delivery
grace without backfilling. They contain 44,577 bins covering
891.54 seconds inside the measurement, with
10 all-missing observed bins. Unobserved outage intervals
are not converted to synthetic bins or treated as complete coverage. The longest
consecutive observed all-missing bin run was
60.0 ms. A delivery gap spanning recovery
must be considered alongside those masks.

Reference fault records:

- At 210.253 s: `gateway ingestion failed: wireless transmitter reference failed: peer_timeout; restart both bridge endpoints`.

There were 0 new gateway USB forwarding errors within the observed,
within-boot status intervals. Maximum dequeued host USB wait across the complete
capture was 120.46 ms against the
unchanged 200 ms guard. A headless run does not establish GUI/video-load
reliability or resolve the separate relay fault.

## Integrity, artifacts and limits

The independent envelope CRC/header audit checked
180,953 complete stored envelopes and
361,687 nested records across warm-up, measurement and drain.
It checked framing lengths, run/batch identities, receiver/transmitter identities,
profile, sensing channel/format and driver flags using the production wire
structures. There were 17 driver-flagged
records across the full capture; raw bytes remain preserved. Host ingestion
performed full CSI decoding and validation during capture. The offline audit
did not alter or recreate CSI sample values.

Raw journals, immutable envelopes/CSVs, status samples, exact configuration,
recovery events and `loss-summary.json` are under
`data/experiments/gateway/loss-15min-20261104T074025Z`. The `analysis-tools/`
subdirectory preserves experiment scripts and their hashes; console logs are
under `.codex-build/loss-15min-20261104/`. The final device
status snapshots record shutdown verification. No earlier recording was changed.

This measures loss for one room placement and headless workload. It does not
establish fall-detection accuracy, tolerance of missing views, continuous
reference reliability or performance under phone-video/GUI load. The recovery
silence is a separate system reliability limitation even if average delivery
exceeds 80 records/s.
